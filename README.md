# Essential — Your Social Media Helper

**Essential turns your ideas into ready-to-post social media content.** It writes the posts, makes the videos, shows you a preview for your approval, then posts to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube for you.

## What it does

Give Essential a topic and it handles the whole job:

1. **Research** — looks into the topic
2. **Write** — writes the posts for each platform
3. **Make** — creates the videos and images
4. **Show you** — gives you a preview
5. **You approve** — you say yes or no
6. **Post** — publishes to your platforms automatically

Every video is at least 15 minutes of spoken content, and each platform's limits are respected (for example, Instagram allows up to 10 minutes).

## What's inside

- **The app** (`apps/`) — the web service and the behind-the-scenes workers
- **The building blocks** (`packages/`) — the parts that do the writing, the videos, the approvals, the publishing, and the built-in AI assistant (Neo)
- **Platform connections** (`adapters/`) — how Essential talks to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube
